Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change my subscription plan?

As a subscription owner, you can easily change your plan by logging in and navigating to the "Billing" tab in your Owner Dashboard.

Can doctors work at multiple clinics?

Yes! Our platform natively supports doctors operating across multiple clinics. They simply select their active clinic upon login.

Is my patient data secure?

Absolutely. We utilize enterprise-grade security on Google Cloud Platform (Firebase) with strict Role-Based Access Control rules.

Can I manage staff leaves?

Yes, the Pro and Enterprise plans include a comprehensive Leave Management module that automatically blocks out unavailable slots.
